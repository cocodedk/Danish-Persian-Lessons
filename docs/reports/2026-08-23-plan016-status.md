# Status report — Plan 016 "Tæl til tyve" (counting 1–20)

Date: 2026-08-23 · Branch: `feat/counting-1-20` · Git commit history is
authoritative for what is committed.

Companion document: [`docs/plans/016-counting-1-20.md`](../plans/016-counting-1-20.md).
This report records what is true on the branch right now, including the parts
that are not finished.

## Shape of the work

### One canonical 1-20 lesson

There is exactly one counting foundation, at `/lesson/taelle`
([`src/pages/CountingScreen.tsx`](../../src/pages/CountingScreen.tsx),
registered in [`src/App.tsx`](../../src/App.tsx#L74)). It teaches every number
from 1 to 20 as a complete card — digit, vocalized word, dansk lydskrift, IPA
and Danish meaning — plus two tap-only exercise rounds at
`/lesson/taelle/ovelse/:kind`.

**No separate 1-10 lesson exists.** The course home
([`src/pages/Home.tsx`](../../src/pages/Home.tsx)), the Tal shelf
([`src/pages/SpeakingHome.tsx`](../../src/pages/SpeakingHome.tsx)) and the child
workshop's number section
([`src/pages/ChildHome.tsx`](../../src/pages/ChildHome.tsx)) are entry points
only: each derives title, summary, range and count from the descriptor
[`src/lessons/countingLesson.ts`](../../src/lessons/countingLesson.ts) and links
into the one lesson. None holds its own range, ordering, title or progress
record. The retired `/tal/tal/:page` URLs replace-navigate to
`countingLesson.path` ([`src/App.tsx`](../../src/App.tsx#L56)).

### `beginnerNumbers` is compatibility data, not a lesson

`beginnerNumbers` in [`src/lessons/numbers.ts`](../../src/lessons/numbers.ts) is
retained unchanged, in its original order and with its original ids, purely so
the reviewed 1-10 audio and the `numberCatalog` export keep exactly the entries
they had before the lesson grew to twenty. It has no route, no lesson surface
and no progress record of its own, and must not be reordered, resized, or
presented as a lesson.

### 11-20 is still blocked

The Persian forms, IPA and dansk lydskrift for **11-20** (یازده … بیست) are
candidates. They are **not approved and not standard**, and remain blocked by
[`AAA-COUNTING-CURRICULUM-SPEC.md`](../specs/AAA-COUNTING-CURRICULUM-SPEC.md)
§6 and [`AAA-QUALITY-BAR.md`](../specs/AAA-QUALITY-BAR.md) Gate A. Their
presence in
[`docs/reviews/content-review-manifest.json`](../reviews/content-review-manifest.json)
is queue membership, not approval.

### Three future rule lessons: contracted, not implemented

[`DESIGN.md`](../../DESIGN.md) (the counting decision, and the four-stage list)
and [`AAA-COUNTING-CURRICULUM-SPEC.md`](../specs/AAA-COUNTING-CURRICULUM-SPEC.md)
§1 and §3 contract **three separate rule lessons** above the foundation:

1. **21-99** — how tens and units combine into one spoken number.
2. **100-900** — how hundreds are formed and joined to the range below.
3. **1,000-9,999** — thousand multipliers 1-9 and composition with the ranges
   below, up to the 9,999 scope boundary.

**None of the three is implemented on this branch.** They are out of scope for
plan 016; no form belonging to them is pre-approved here, and `/lesson/taelle`
must not be extended in place to cover them. Each is specified to carry its own
route, its own add-only store and its own id namespace.

## Implementation fixes already present on the branch

- **`ChoiceExercise` respects `showsPron`.** The pronunciation line is now
  rendered only when the question does not opt out
  ([`src/components/ChoiceExercise.tsx`](../../src/components/ChoiceExercise.tsx#L101)),
  matching `ReviewSession`. The `betydning` round sets `showsPron: false` — the
  dansk lydskrift would otherwise spell the answer. Covered by a test in
  [`ChoiceExercise.test.tsx`](../../src/components/ChoiceExercise.test.tsx#L103).
- **Counting exercise row shape fixed.**
  [`buildCountingQuestions`](../../src/lessons/countingExercises.ts) builds each
  question from `row.word` for `entry`, `itemId` and `answerId`, so a question's
  identity, its progress key and its reveal all refer to the same entry.
  Distractors are sound-safe neighbours (`alike()` rejects a shared Danish name,
  IPA or dansk lydskrift); a pool too small to fill four choices throws rather
  than shipping a short round.
- **`CountingScreen` progress honesty.** A tap on a number only browses — it
  updates the detail strip and writes nothing. Marking a number done is a
  separate, deliberate button, so the "N af 20 gennemgået eller øvet" line
  reflects what the learner actually did.
- **Celebration honesty.** The celebration is scoped to `justMarked`, the single
  word whose completion was earned on this visit: no second tick beside it, and
  none left behind when browsing moves on.
- **Audio honesty.** The screen adds no audio affordance of its own. Playback
  appears only through `DetailStrip` →
  [`OptionalAudioControl`](../../src/components/OptionalAudioControl.tsx), which
  renders nothing unless an approved clip exists for that entry — so no control
  is shown for the unreviewed 11-20 words.
- **Unknown-kind redirect.**
  [`CountingExerciseScreen`](../../src/pages/CountingExerciseScreen.tsx)
  validates `:kind` with `isCountingExerciseKind` and replace-navigates to a
  stable route when it does not match.
- **Measured bottom-nav clearance.** The fixed `AreaNav` measures itself and
  publishes its border-box height (safe-area padding included) as
  `--area-nav-block-size`; `.app-content` reserves exactly that
  ([`AppChrome.css`](../../src/components/AppChrome.css)). The literal fallback
  applies only before/without that measurement and deliberately over-reserves,
  so the nav can never cover the last line of content. `AreaNav` labels are
  single words and no longer break mid-word.
- **CSS under budget.** No new stylesheet was added; the screens reuse the
  existing vocab/alphabet/DetailStrip classes. The reviewer run's build produced
  `dist/assets/index-*.css` at 49,961 bytes, under the 50,000-byte ceiling in
  [`scripts/verify.sh`](../../scripts/verify.sh) — with only about 39 bytes of
  headroom, so any new rule needs a matching removal.

## Standing diagnosis: `talkAudioReady()` is deterministic

`talkAudioReady()` is computed from the committed `launchTalkClipIds` list plus
[`src/audio/approved.generated.json`](../../src/audio/approved.generated.json),
and all 97 launch clips resolve. The gate is therefore open for every checkout
of `main`, on every machine — this is not environment-dependent. `main` shows
the "Tal" tab in `AreaNav` and opens the speaking front door at `#/`. The
committed baselines were last regenerated on 2026-08-09 (`1ddaba9`), before the
speaking audio was published (`b1ee3ef`), so every screenshot in the suite
predates that change. That single published-audio milestone — not hundreds of
independently drifted screens — is why the whole baseline set differs.

## Other environment note

`npm run` injects a local Socket Firewall HTTPS proxy, through which
Playwright's web-server health check routes and times out after 60 s. e2e and
verify commands need a `NO_PROXY=127.0.0.1,localhost
no_proxy=127.0.0.1,localhost` prefix.
