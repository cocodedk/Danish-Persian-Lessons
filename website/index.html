Persian on top, Danish underneath

Learn to read Persian, starting from the alphabet

You already speak the language. What you never got was the thirty-two letters and the handful of marks that hold them together.

  • اَ
  • اِ
  • اُ
  • آ
  • او
  • ای

Six marks. Nearly every vowel in Persian is one of them, and they are the difference between a row of shapes and a word you know.

Who this is for

  • You speak Persian, you just don't read it

    Persian at home, Danish everywhere else. A message arrives from your grandmother and you hand the phone to someone older. You're not starting at zero here. You already have the language, and that's the part that takes years.

  • You read, but every line costs you

    A name, a street sign, a headline: fine, given a moment. A whole page is work. We go back to the marks most people skip and stay there until reading them stops being a decision.

How it works

  1. 1

    Letters, and all four of their shapes

    A Persian letter looks different at the start of a word, in the middle, at the end and standing on its own. You meet all four at once (ب ـب ـبـ بـ), so none of them catches you out later.

  2. 2

    The vowel marks, in red

    اَ اِ اُ sit above and below the letters, and ordinary print drops them after first grade. You learn them first. They are the reason you can say a word you have never seen before.

  3. 3

    Real words from the first-grade primer

    The words Iranian children read on their first day of school: آب، بابا، نان. Short, common, and worth having on their own.

Nothing leaves your phone

There is no account and no sign-up. Your progress is written to your own browser, and it stays there.

  • No account, no email, no password.
  • No analytics. No tracking. Only the fonts are fetched from an outside server.
  • Progress is kept in the browser's own storage (localStorage).
  • Clear your browser data and it's gone. There is no copy on a server to lose.
